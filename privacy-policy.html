Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Groupverse ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website and use our Service. Please read this policy carefully. By using Groupverse, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to keep data collection minimal, since Groupverse does not require account creation or sign-up to browse or join listed groups. That said, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our site, such as pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and click patterns, typically collected through cookies or analytics tools.
  • Device and Technical Data: Information such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, and general geographic location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS location).
  • Voluntarily Submitted Information: If you use our "Submit Group" feature to list a WhatsApp or Telegram group, we collect the information you provide, such as the group name, description, category, and invite link. We do not require your name, email, or phone number to submit a listing unless explicitly requested for verification purposes.
  • Communications: If you contact us directly (for support, a takedown request, or feedback), we retain the content of that communication along with any contact details you choose to share.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Groupverse does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile information, or Telegram username through our Service. When you click a listing and join a third-party group or channel, any information you share within that group (such as your phone number or profile photo, as visible to other group members) is governed by WhatsApp's or Telegram's own privacy practices, not ours. We have no access to, and no control over, what happens inside a third-party group once you join it.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like most websites, Groupverse may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our site. These may include:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand aggregate visitor behavior (e.g., via tools like Google Analytics), helping us improve site performance and content organization.
  • Advertising cookies: If third-party advertising is displayed on our site, ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing activity across websites.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect certain site features but will not prevent you from browsing listings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Groupverse website and its listings.
  • Analyze site traffic and usage trends to improve navigation, category organization, and content relevance.
  • Review and moderate user-submitted group listings for compliance with our Terms of Use.
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, and takedown or removal requests.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or illegal activity on our platform.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to help operate our Service, such as web hosting providers, analytics platforms, and content delivery networks. These providers may have access to limited technical data (such as IP address or usage statistics) solely to perform services on our behalf, and are contractually or technically restricted from using this data for unrelated purposes. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Additionally, our Service links to third-party platforms, including WhatsApp and Telegram. These platforms have their own independent privacy policies, and we encourage you to review WhatsApp's and Telegram's respective privacy policies before joining any group or channel, since Groupverse has no visibility into or responsibility for data practices once you leave our site.

6. Advertising

If Groupverse displays advertisements, these may be served by third-party ad networks that use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads relevant to your interests based on your browsing activity. We do not control the specific ads shown or the data practices of third-party ad networks. You may be able to opt out of interest-based advertising through your browser settings or industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance's consumer choice page.

7. Data Retention

We retain usage data and analytics information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, such as improving site performance and security. User-submitted group listings are retained until removed by the submitter, flagged and removed by our moderation process, or found to be inactive or in violation of our Terms of Use.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the Service at your own risk with respect to any inherent security limitations of internet-based platforms.

9. Children's Privacy

Groupverse is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under the applicable age, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of information we hold about you, as well as the right to object to certain data uses or request data portability. Since Groupverse collects minimal personal information by design, many of these requests will primarily apply to voluntarily submitted content (such as a group listing) or communications you've had with us. To exercise any applicable rights, please contact us using the details on our homepage.

11. International Users

Groupverse may be accessed by users from various countries. By using our Service, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in locations outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection laws than your home jurisdiction. We take reasonable steps to ensure information is handled in accordance with this policy regardless of where it is processed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will indicate the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our homepage or via our support channels.
