Group Safety Tips

Joining public WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels is generally safe, but a few precautions go a long way.

Before You Join

  • Check the group description and category to confirm it matches what you're looking for.
  • Be cautious of groups promising unrealistic returns (investment "guarantees," free money, prize claims).
  • Prefer listings with a Verified badge where extra review has been done, though this isn't a full guarantee of safety.

What Not to Share Inside a Group

  • OTPs, passwords, or banking PINs — no legitimate group or admin needs these.
  • Personal documents like Aadhaar, PAN, or ID scans.
  • Your exact home address or daily schedule.

Privacy Settings

On WhatsApp, you can adjust who sees your profile photo, last seen, and status from Settings → Privacy before joining unfamiliar groups. On Telegram, similar privacy controls are available under Settings → Privacy and Security.

If Something Feels Wrong

Leave the group immediately, and use the report (flag) icon on the Groupverse listing, or email abuse@groupverse.co.in. See our full disclaimer for how listings are reviewed.
