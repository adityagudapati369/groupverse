Telegram's Unique Features for Communities

Telegram offers features less common on WhatsApp — larger group and channel size limits, bots for automation, and public channel discoverability through Telegram's own search. This makes it especially popular for large-scale news distribution, crypto trading communities, and tech-focused discussion where broadcast reach matters more than intimate group conversation.

Tips for Joining Telegram Communities Safely

As with any messaging platform, review a channel or group's description before joining, and never share your Telegram account credentials, two-factor authentication codes, or financial information with anyone in a group.
