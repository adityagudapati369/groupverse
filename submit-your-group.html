Submit Your WhatsApp Group or Telegram Channel — Free

Adding your WhatsApp group or Telegram channel to Groupverse takes a few minutes and standard listings are completely free.

Submission Steps

Click "Submit Group" on the Groupverse homepage, enter your group or channel name, invite link, category, country, and language, add an optional description and logo to help your listing stand out, then submit — most listings are manually reviewed and go live shortly after.

Submission Requirements

You must have the right to share the invite link, and the group must not promote illegal activity, hate speech, or misrepresented adult content. See our full Terms of Use for the complete submission policy, including our stance on prohibited content categories.

Writing a Description That Attracts the Right Members

A clear, specific description significantly improves the quality of members who join — vague descriptions attract mismatched members who leave quickly, while specific descriptions (mentioning your group's exact focus, posting frequency, and any rules) attract genuinely interested, longer-staying members. Mentioning what makes your community distinct from similar groups also helps it stand out in category browsing.

Want Faster Review or More Visibility?

Optional paid add-ons — Priority Review, Verified Badge, and Sponsored placement — are available. See our Advertise page for details on each option and current pricing.

After You're Listed

Keep your invite link active — if it expires or resets (which happens automatically once a group hits its member limit, or if an admin manually resets it), update your listing by contacting us via the Contact page. Listings with consistently broken links may be removed during periodic quality reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to submit my group or channel?
Yes, standard listings are completely free — optional paid features for extra visibility are available separately.

How long does the review process take?
Standard review typically completes within a reasonable timeframe; Priority Review is available for faster turnaround.

What should I include in my group description?
Be specific about your group's focus, posting frequency, and any rules — vague descriptions attract mismatched members.

What happens if my invite link expires after listing?
Update your listing by contacting us via the Contact page — links with persistent issues may be removed during quality reviews.

Can I submit both a WhatsApp and Telegram community?
Yes, submit each separately through the "Submit Group" button, selecting the correct platform for each.
