Submit Your Group or Channel — Free

Adding your WhatsApp group or Telegram channel to Groupverse takes a few minutes and standard listings are completely free.

Go to Submit Group →

Submission Steps

  1. Click "Submit Group" on the Groupverse homepage.
  2. Enter your group or channel name, invite link, category, country, and language.
  3. Add an optional description and logo to help your listing stand out.
  4. Submit — most listings are manually reviewed and go live shortly after.

Submission Requirements

You must have the right to share the invite link, and the group must not promote illegal activity, hate speech, or misrepresented adult content. See our full Terms of Use for the complete submission policy.

Want Faster Review or More Visibility?

Optional paid add-ons — Priority Review, Verified Badge, and Sponsored placement — are available. See our Advertise page for details.

After You're Listed

Keep your invite link active — if it expires or resets, update your listing by contacting us via the Contact page.
