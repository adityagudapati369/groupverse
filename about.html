About Groupverse

Groupverse is a free, community-driven directory of WhatsApp and Telegram group and channel invite links. Our goal is simple: help people find active, relevant communities to join — across categories like technology, jobs, travel, finance, entertainment, and dozens more — without any sign-up or cost.

What We Do

We list publicly shared invite links submitted by group and channel admins, organized by category, country, and language so you can find communities that match your interests quickly.

What We Don't Do

We don't create, own, or moderate the content inside any listed group or channel. Each community is run independently by its own admin. See our Terms of Use for details on submission rules and moderation.

Why Groupverse

New groups are added daily by the community, listings are free, and there's no account required to browse or join. If you run a group or channel, you can submit it for listing directly from the homepage.
