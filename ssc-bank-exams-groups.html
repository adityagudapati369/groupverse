What You'll Find in This Category

These communities support aspirants preparing for SSC exams (CGL, CHSL, MTS, and others) and banking sector exams (IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, RBI exams) — covering quantitative aptitude and reasoning practice, English language preparation, general awareness compilation, and exam notification alerts across these various recruitment cycles.

Common Topics Discussed

Members discuss quantitative aptitude shortcuts and practice questions, reasoning ability problem-solving techniques, English language grammar and comprehension practice, banking and general awareness current affairs, and exam pattern changes or notification updates across the many SSC and banking exam cycles that run through the year.

Why Aspirants Join These Groups

SSC and banking exams share substantially overlapping syllabi (quant, reasoning, English, general awareness), making shared practice material and strategy discussion efficient across multiple simultaneous exam preparations. Given how frequently new notifications are released across various SSC and banking exams, group-based alert consolidation also saves significant time compared to tracking each recruiting body's website individually.

Tips Before You Join

Given the shared syllabus across SSC and banking exams, prioritize groups with genuinely active practice and doubt-solving over ones that are mostly notification-forwarding without substantive discussion. Verify quantitative or reasoning shortcut techniques shared in groups by practicing them yourself before relying on them during actual exam time pressure.

Understanding the SSC and Banking Exam Landscape

SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level) and CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level) cover various government department postings, while IBPS conducts common recruitment exams for multiple public sector banks' PO (Probationary Officer) and Clerk positions, alongside SBI's separate recruitment process. Given the multi-tier exam structure common to most of these (Tier 1 prelims, Tier 2 mains, sometimes an interview or skill test), aspirants often prepare across an extended timeline spanning multiple exam attempts and recruitment cycles within a year.
