Why Teaching Aspirants Join These Groups

Teaching eligibility exams share substantial common ground in pedagogy and child development theory, making peer discussion efficient for aspirants sometimes preparing for multiple state TETs alongside CTET simultaneously. Given that many aspirants are already working as teachers or in related roles, group-based preparation offers flexible, on-the-go study support around existing work schedules.

Tips Before You Join

CTET and various State TETs have different exact syllabi and cutoff patterns despite overlapping core concepts, so verify which specific exam a group focuses on before assuming content applies directly to your target exam. For recruitment notifications shared in groups, cross-check details directly through official state education department or CTET websites before applying.

Understanding CTET and State TETs

CTET, conducted by CBSE, qualifies candidates for teaching positions in central government schools and is also accepted by several states, while individual State TETs are required for state government school recruitment in their respective states. Both exams have two papers — Paper 1 for teaching classes 1-5 and Paper 2 for classes 6-8 — and candidates can attempt one or both depending on their intended teaching level.
