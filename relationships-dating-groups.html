What You'll Find in This Category

These communities range from general relationship advice groups to dating experience discussion and friendship-building spaces for meeting new people. Expect advice threads on relationship challenges, discussion of modern dating app experiences, and general conversation about building and maintaining healthy relationships.

Common Topics Discussed

Members discuss relationship advice and conflict navigation, dating app experiences and etiquette, communication strategies for partners, long-distance relationship challenges, and general discussion about modern dating culture. Some groups focus specifically on friendship-building for people looking to expand their social circle.

Why People Join These Groups

Relationship questions can feel awkward to ask friends or family, and anonymous or semi-anonymous peer groups offer a more comfortable space for honest discussion. These communities also normalize common relationship struggles by showing that many people face similar challenges, which can be reassuring during a difficult period.

Important Safety Guidance

If you meet someone through a relationship or dating-focused group with intent to meet in person, apply the same safety precautions as any online dating situation — video call before meeting, meet in a public place for a first meeting, tell a friend or family member your plans, and trust your instincts if something feels off. Never share financial information or send money to someone you've only interacted with through a group, as romance-related scams are a genuine and common risk.

A Note on Respectful Discussion

Healthy relationship discussion should respect all genders and relationship structures without generalizing negatively about any group. If you encounter harassment or disrespectful content in a group, report it via the flag icon on the listing or email abuse@groupverse.co.in.
