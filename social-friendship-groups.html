What You'll Find in This Category

These communities range from general casual conversation groups to interest-based friendship circles (bonding over shared hobbies) and city-specific social groups that occasionally organize in-person meetups. Expect casual daily conversation, introduction threads for new members, and shared-interest discussion that naturally builds friendships over time.

Common Topics Discussed

Members engage in general casual conversation, introduction and "get to know you" threads, shared hobby or interest discussion that brings people together, local meetup coordination for city-specific groups, and general social support during day-to-day life.

Why People Join These Groups

Making new friends as an adult is notoriously harder than in school or college, since natural social opportunities decrease significantly, and these groups provide a structured way to meet new people with at least one shared context (the group's specific focus). For people who've relocated to a new city, these communities can also provide an initial social network before building deeper offline connections.

Safety When Building New Friendships Online

As with any online social space, apply reasonable caution when transitioning from group chat to one-on-one conversation, especially before meeting in person — video calls first, public meeting locations, and telling someone else your plans are sensible precautions. Be skeptical of anyone in the group asking for money or personal financial information, regardless of how genuine the friendship feels.

Finding Your Specific Community

General social groups can feel less engaging than interest-based ones, since shared context tends to build friendship faster than pure random conversation. If you have a specific hobby or interest, our other category pages — from Books & Literature to Gaming & Esports to Fitness & Bodybuilding — often make better starting points for genuine friendship-building around something you're already passionate about.
