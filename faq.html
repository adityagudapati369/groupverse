Frequently Asked Questions

What is Groupverse?

Groupverse is a free directory of WhatsApp and Telegram group and channel invite links, organized by category, country, and language, so you can discover active communities without needing an account.

How do I join a WhatsApp group using a link?

Tap "Join Group" or "Follow" on any listing. This opens the invite link directly in WhatsApp or Telegram (or a browser prompt to open the app), where you can confirm joining.

Is it free to submit my group or channel?

Yes, standard listings are free. Optional paid add-ons like Priority Review, Verified Badge, or Sponsored placement are available if you want extra visibility — see the "Submit Group" button on the homepage for details.

Why did a group link stop working?

WhatsApp and Telegram invite links can expire if the group hits its member limit, the admin resets the link, or the group is deleted. Report a broken link using the flag icon on the listing, or email abuse@groupverse.co.in.

Does Groupverse moderate the groups themselves?

We review submissions before listing them, but we don't control or monitor ongoing activity inside third-party groups. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

How do I report an inappropriate group?

Use the report (flag) icon on any listing, or email abuse@groupverse.co.in. We aim to review reports within 48 hours.
