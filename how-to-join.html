How to Join a Group on Groupverse

Joining a WhatsApp Group or Channel

  1. Browse or search for a group using the category, country, or keyword filters on the homepage.
  2. Tap the green Join Group or Follow button on the listing.
  3. WhatsApp will open (or prompt you to open the app) showing a preview of the group or channel.
  4. Tap Join Chat or Follow inside WhatsApp to confirm.

Joining a Telegram Group or Channel

  1. Tap the blue Join Group or Subscribe button on the listing.
  2. Telegram will open the invite link showing the group or channel name and member count.
  3. Tap Join to confirm membership.

Staying Safe

Never share personal or financial details inside a group unless you fully trust the source. Groupverse lists publicly submitted invite links and does not moderate ongoing activity inside third-party communities — see our Privacy Policy for details. If something feels off, leave the group and use the report (flag) icon on the listing.

Link Not Working?

Invite links can expire when a group reaches its member limit or the admin resets it. Report broken links using the flag icon, or email abuse@groupverse.co.in.
