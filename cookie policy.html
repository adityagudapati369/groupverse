Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

What We Use

Groupverse uses Google Analytics to understand site traffic (pages viewed, general location, device type). We also store a local cooldown timestamp in your browser's localStorage to prevent spam submissions — this data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

No Advertising Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell browsing data to advertisers.

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may not affect core site functionality, since we don't rely on cookies for browsing or joining groups.
