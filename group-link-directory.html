What Is a WhatsApp & Telegram Group Link Directory?

A group link directory is a website that collects and organizes publicly shared WhatsApp group and Telegram channel invite links, making it easier to discover active communities without manually searching social media or forums.

How Groupverse Works

Group and channel admins submit their invite links to Groupverse along with a category, country, and language. Submissions go through a basic manual review before appearing on the site, organized so visitors can filter by topic, region, or keyword using the tools on the homepage.

Why Use a Directory Instead of Searching Manually

Group invite links aren't indexed well by regular search engines and are often shared informally on forums or social posts, where they go stale quickly as groups fill up or admins reset links. A maintained directory aims to keep listings reasonably current and organized by real categories, saving the time it would take to manually track down relevant, active communities across scattered sources.

What Makes a Good Group Link Directory

A useful directory should offer clear categorization, reasonably current listings, transparent submission and review processes, and some mechanism for reporting broken or problematic links. Groupverse aims to meet these standards through category and language filtering, a manual review process for submissions, and a report (flag) icon on every listing for community-driven quality control.

Staying Safe on Any Directory

No directory — including this one — can fully vet ongoing activity inside a third-party group after listing. Read our disclaimer and joining guide before sharing personal information in any group you join, and always apply the same caution you would with any online community you're new to.

Running a group or channel? Use the "Submit Group" button on the homepage, or see our submission guide for step-by-step details, and our featured listing options for extra visibility beyond standard free listings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a group link directory?
A website that collects and organizes publicly shared WhatsApp group and Telegram channel invite links for easier discovery.

How does Groupverse review submissions?
Submissions go through a basic manual review before appearing on the site, checked against our Terms of Use.

Can a directory guarantee a group is safe or active?
No, ongoing activity inside third-party groups can't be fully vetted after listing — apply normal online safety caution.

How do I report a broken or problematic link?
Use the report (flag) icon on any listing, or email abuse@groupverse.co.in.

Is it free to be listed in this directory?
Yes, standard listings are free — optional paid features for extra visibility are available separately.
